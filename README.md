# 🟩 SimpleProtect – Your build matters!

**SimpleProtect** is a lightweight and fully server-side **Fabric** mod that brings a simple, emerald-block-based protection system to your Minecraft world.  
Farm emeralds, craft them into blocks, and use them to safeguard your builds from griefers.  

Perfect for survival servers, SMPs, and creative builders who want peace of mind.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🟩 Protect your builds using emerald blocks  
- 🛡️ Create grief-proof zones around your emerald block protections  
- 👥 Share access by adding friends to your protection list  
- ❌ Remove players from access at any time  
- 📁 Per-protection data stored in easy-to-read JSON files  
- 🌍 100% server-side — no client mod required!  

---

## 🔧 Commands

- `/sprotection add <player>` – Grant a player access to your protection  
- `/sprotection remove <player>` – Remove a player’s access to your protection  

---

## 🧩 Requirements

- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/)  
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)  

---

## 🌟 Why SimpleProtect?

Because **your build matters!**  
SimpleProtect keeps your world safe from griefers without complicated claim systems or confusing GUIs.  

Just place emerald blocks, protect your area, and build with confidence.
